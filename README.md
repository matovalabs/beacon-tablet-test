# Beacon tablet test

Beacon is an early test of a learning app for children in grades 1 to 12 (ages 6 to 18), made by Matova. Children practice reading, math, science and social studies. This page is for the families we invited to try it.

Beacon sends nothing to us or to anyone else. Your child's results stay on your tablet.

## New in version 0.8.0

- The guides dress for the seasons. Each wears a small accessory that walks with them, such as a scarf in winter or a sun hat in summer, and their home scenes get a few decorations.
- In the Grown-up area, under Settings and privacy, you can choose holidays your family celebrates. There are 13 to pick from, and all are off unless you turn them on. A chosen holiday's look shows for a few days around the date.
- Nothing appears on reading or question screens, and everything stays on the tablet.

## New in version 0.7.0

- A small status pill at the top of the screen shows this week's reading days and, during a quest, which question your child is on. Tap it to see the week, the total reading days and today's keepsake. It never moves while your child reads or answers.
- The guides greet your child warmly when they come back, and cheer when a text is finished. They are never sad.
- The optional daily reminder (Android app) can suggest the hour your child usually reads, takes turns between a few kind sentences, and pauses itself if it goes unused for a week. You choose; nothing changes by itself. Everything stays on the tablet.

## New in version 0.6.1

- The guides now walk with legs, arms and hands that move on their own. One foot always stays on the ground, so they step instead of sliding. Tally the crab scuttles sideways on her legs and taps her shells with a claw.

## New in version 0.6.0

- Every grade from 1 to 12, with one level per grade (12 levels instead of 10).
- Four subjects: reading, math, science and social studies. A grown-up picks which subjects a child has, and the child chooses the subject each day.
- New texts for grades 11 and 12 and the first science texts for grades 1 to 10. Texts are early drafts that a teacher has not reviewed yet; tell us if anything looks wrong.
- Smoother walking for the guides and new celebration clips, a press and hold word helper, level labels on the progress chart, and fixes from our own testing.

## Install on an Android or Amazon Fire tablet

1. On the tablet, tap this link to download the app: [Beacon-test-0.8.0.apk](https://github.com/matovalabs/beacon-tablet-test/releases/download/v0.8.0-preview/Beacon-test-0.8.0.apk) (46 MB).
2. Open the downloaded file. The tablet asks whether to allow installs from the app you used to open it (for example Chrome or Files). Allow it, go back, and tap Install. On a Fire tablet the setting is under Settings > Security & Privacy > Apps from Unknown Sources.
3. If a warning says the app comes from an unknown developer, tap More details, then Install anyway. It is our test app, and it is not in the Play Store yet.
4. Open Beacon from the home screen. You can turn the install setting back off afterwards.

Already have an older version? Install 0.8.0 the same way. It replaces the old version and keeps your child's progress.

## Use it on an iPad

1. On the iPad, open this link in Safari: https://matovalabs.github.io/beacon-tablet-test/
2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow, at the top). If you do not see Add to Home Screen, tap View More. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
3. From now on, open Beacon from its icon on the Home Screen, not from Safari. Your child's progress is saved with that icon. In a Safari tab, Safari may clear saved progress after about a week without a visit.

On an iPad, Beacon needs the internet to open. It still sends nothing to us. It picks up new versions by itself and keeps your child's progress. The daily reminder is only in the Android app.

## When you are done

Open Beacon, tap Grown-ups, then Settings and privacy, then Clear data. On an Android tablet, deleting the app does the same. We hold no copy.
